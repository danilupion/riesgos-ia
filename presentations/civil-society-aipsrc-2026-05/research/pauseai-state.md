# PauseAI state — research for civil-society-aipsrc-2026-05

> Compiled 2026-05-14 via thorough web crawl of pauseai.info, chapter sites, press coverage.

## TL;DR

PauseAI is a three-year-old global grassroots movement founded May 2023 in Utrecht by Joep Meindertsma, advocating for an international treaty pausing frontier general AI development until safety is provable. By May 2026 it has grown to ~600+ members in a federation of 16+ national chapters with paid Chapter Directors in strategically important locations, a Global executive team in Brussels (CEO Maxime Fournes, Organizing Director Irina Tavera, Comms Director Jonathan Moody), and a 501(c)(3) US affiliate (Holly Elmore, Founder & Executive Director). It pushed for and arguably catalysed the UK Bletchley Park AI Safety Summit, drives the Future of Life Institute's superintelligence statement (now 30,000-70,000+ signatures depending on count, with Hinton, Bengio, Wozniak, Branson, Mary Robinson, Steve Bannon, Prince Harry signing), and has notched concrete civil-society wins: 60 UK politicians signing an open letter that forced Google DeepMind to grant the UK AISI pre-deployment access to Gemini 3 Pro; 74 Congressional meetings in PauseCon DC April 2026; visible role helping defeat the federal AI preemption moratorium in the US Senate 99-1; the largest-ever AI-safety protest (300+ in London, Feb 2026); and a PauseCon Brussels conference inside the European Parliament with Stuart Russell and four named MEPs. For an LL.M. policy audience, PauseAI is the textbook case of a small civil-society actor closing the gap between high public concern (~70% want a slowdown) and political action through theory-of-change-driven federated organising.

## PauseAI International (pauseai.info)

### Strategy / theory of change

PauseAI explicitly calls itself "the activation layer" between public concern and political action: "Good policy proposals for AI governance already exist. Public concern about AI is high and growing. But concern does not automatically become political action."
(https://pauseai.info/theory-of-change)

Five conditions they argue must be met simultaneously for a global pause:

1. Political will in major-power governments
2. A technical and governance framework ready to execute
3. A triggering window (event-driven) to break political inertia
4. Elite consensus that pausing is responsible
5. A public mandate visible enough to reward signing politically

PauseAI explicitly focuses on conditions 1, 3 and 5; policy organisations (FLI, CAIS, GovAI) handle 2 and 4. (https://pauseai.info/theory-of-change)

Tactics: organiser training via PauseCon, a federation structure ("shared campaigns on shared timelines, with shared messaging and shared infrastructure"), grassroots lobbying (Email Builder, two coordinated global protests per year), coalition building. (https://pauseai.info/theory-of-change)

### Current demands (verbatim)

Mission, revised September 2025: "We call on the governments of the world to sign an international treaty implementing a pause on the training of the most powerful general AI systems, until we know how to build them safely."
(https://pauseai.substack.com/p/a-new-phase-for-pauseai)

Three concrete components of the proposed treaty (https://pauseai.info/proposal):

1. Establish an **International AI Safety Agency (AISA)** modelled on the IAEA, with authority over major training runs and deployments, GPU tracking, whistleblower incentives, energy monitoring and facility inspections.
2. Prohibit training of more powerful general AI systems until provable safety.
3. Restrict deployment of models with dangerous capabilities; AI weights remain "strictly under the stewardship of the AISA" until veto-power coalition agrees deployment is safe.

Supporting policy asks: ban training on copyrighted material; criminal liability for model creators whose models are misused; longer-term, restrict publication of capability-advancing algorithms.

Framing references PauseAI uses (https://pauseai.info/proposal):

- **IAEA** — model for the AISA
- **"CERN for AI"** — international institution licensed to conduct large-scale experiments above the moratorium threshold
- **Asilomar 1975** — historical precedent for voluntary scientist moratoria on recombinant DNA
- **Montreal Protocol, Biological Weapons Convention, NPT** — feasibility precedents (https://pauseai.info/feasibility)

### Sam Altman attack — distinguishing PauseAI from StopAI

On April 10/15, 2026, a 20-year-old named Daniel Moreno-Gama allegedly attempted to firebomb Altman's San Francisco home. The suspect had posted 34 messages on PauseAI's Discord two years prior, never participated in campaigns. PauseAI's statement (https://pauseai.info/statement-sam-altman-attack-2026):

- "PauseAI unequivocally condemns this attack and all forms of violence, intimidation and harassment."
- "We wish safety and peace to Sam Altman, his family, and everyone affected."
- "He had no role in PauseAI, participated in no campaigns, attended no events."
- "Advocating safety, regulation and democratic oversight of powerful technologies is a legitimate and necessary part of public life."

Critical distinction (https://fortune.com/2026/04/15/pause-ai-and-stop-ai-meet-the-anti-ai-groups-facing-questions-after-the-attack-on-sam-altman/):

- **StopAI** (founded 2024 by Sam Kirchner and Guido Reichstadter, both ex-PauseAI) split off over willingness to break the law via civil disobedience.
- Holly Elmore verbatim: "our stance on violence has always been incredibly clear."

## Framing note for Dani

For an LL.M. audience asking what civil society does in AI policy, PauseAI is unusually documentable because they publish a structured theory of change, a track-record page, donor updates with hard numbers, and verbatim Congressional/MEP/MP engagement. The case study works at three levels:

1. **Catalytic** — Bletchley summit chain reaction
2. **Transparency-forcing** — DeepMind letter → AISI pre-deployment access
3. **Legislative shaping** — 99-1 federal preemption defeat, SB 53 support, AI Risk Evaluation Act sponsorship pressure

The London/Brussels/DC PauseCon series is the cleanest concrete pattern of grassroots → legislative engagement in current AI governance.
