"So, the full answer to the question in the title.

Has the era of state control of frontier AI begun?

verification, without public criteria — and structurally ineffective

It is the wrong control. And that is exactly the opportunity: the taboo

govern the frontier, but how. And the window to demand the right version
— treaty, thresholds, verification — is now, while the architecture is
